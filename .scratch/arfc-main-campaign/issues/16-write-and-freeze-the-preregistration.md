## Question

The destination. Write the preregistration from the map's closed tickets, one section per
protocol §6 item (the Notes list which ticket decided each), plus the reporting commitments the
checklist's last item names: the assignment-integrity rate per cell, the failure-cost share per
arm, the bypass-attempt taxonomy, raw gate-exit counts with uncertainty, and the API-condition
log.

It cites, never restates: the pin and the `campaign.json` digests from
[Freeze prompts, pin code, and dry-run](13-freeze-pin-and-dry-run.md), the published arm diffs,
the pre-run parity output, and the statistical plan.

**Where it lives** (`<ai_rfc>/docs/`, next to `experiment-protocol.md`, or a PANTHER spec) and
its form (OSF's template or the protocol's own checklist) are decided here. It is committed by
explicit path and frozen: a later change is a dated amendment, never an edit. **Reached when**
every §6 checkbox is ticked with a pointer to its ticket, the document is committed, and a final
$0 dry-run of the frozen campaign passes. The OSF submission and the launch are out of scope.

Also update the map's Decisions so far and close the map.

The hypothesis section is
[What are the hypotheses](01-what-are-the-hypotheses-and-primary-metrics.md)'s resolution: H1–H4,
Holm, the secondary and descriptive lists, and the definitions. It also states the departure from
protocol §1 ([Does the main campaign include arm C](17-does-the-main-campaign-include-arm-c.md)).
